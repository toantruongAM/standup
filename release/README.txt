StandUp
=======
Sitting all day is bad for you. Every 50 minutes, StandUp freezes your screen
for a 2-minute walking break and shows you a funny picture.

Needs Windows 10 or 11.


INSTALL
Run StandUp-Setup.exe and click Install. No administrator rights needed.
Then choose a passcode. StandUp runs from the tray (near the clock, it may be
under the ^ arrow) and starts with Windows.

The installer isn't code-signed, so Windows may warn you. If you see "Windows
protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. If Smart App Control is
turned on, Windows may block it completely.


DURING A BREAK
- Touching the keyboard or mouse restarts the break timer. Hands off!
- Press Win+L to lock the PC and walk away. The break keeps counting.
- Need a moment? Look for the small dim "snooze" along the bottom of your main
  screen. It gives you 2 more minutes, and you get 2 snoozes per break.

The work timer starts again whenever you turn on, wake or unlock the PC.
Breaks wait during a PowerPoint slide show. They do NOT wait during Zoom or
Teams screen sharing.


SETTINGS (right-click the tray icon)
Settings     Work, break and snooze times, your passcode, your own pictures,
             and an optional timer on the lock screen (needs admin approval)
History      Charts of your breaks over time
Exit         Needs your passcode


UNINSTALL
Exit StandUp from the tray, then remove it in Windows Settings > Apps >
Installed apps.


FORGOT YOUR PASSCODE?
End StandUp in Task Manager, delete this file, and start StandUp again:
%LOCALAPPDATA%\StandUp\settings.ini
That resets your settings but keeps your history.
